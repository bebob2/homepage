My CS Experience

Personal

Prior to my CS classes in school, I had already been creating simple programs and games for a "long" time already. I've always found computers interesting and started to play around with each and every setting I could find. After finding out about VM's on YouTube, I couldn't stop installing different Linux distros and Windows versions and playing around with those. Furthermore I came across game development and started using Unity to create all sorts of games (though mostly 2D). And Video Editing is another hobby of mine. Even before I had my first laptop/PC, I used to create animations and videos (and not very good ones) on my phone using every free app I could find. Later I transitioned to DaVinci Resolve (on Windows) and have since received three certifications by the company behind that software (Blackmagic Design) because the certifications were free and I had some spare time. I've even created a preset that I published and that has actually been used by other people.

School

I had CS classes in school from grade 10 up until the very end (grade 12). During all these three years I've had the same teacher, and we've had these classes at almost the same time and day every year. There I mostly learned about Python, automata, databases and lots more.

CS50x

"CS50, Harvard University’s introduction to the intellectual enterprises of computer science and the art of programming." This is an awesome course available online free of charge even with a free certificate.

I started CS50x during school but stopped, so that I could focus on school a little more. I have continued and finished the last Problem Set during my break between my first and second university semesters. So now I have a CS50x certificate from Harvard University free of charge. And I've already gotten my next one (CS50P - CS50’s Introduction to Programming with Python) by now.
